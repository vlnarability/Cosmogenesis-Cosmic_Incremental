     1→3. Cellular Stage
     2→3.1 Overview
     3→The Cellular Stage represents the beginning of your evolutionary journey, where players control a
     4→single microscopic cell competing for survival in primordial waters. This stage focuses on resource
     5→collection (glucose, protein, lipid, elements), organelle acquisition, and cell division. The choices made
     6→here fundamentally shape how your cell operates and what evolutionary paths become available in
     7→later stages. Players must balance energy production, movement capabilities, defense mechanisms,
     8→and reproductive capacity to thrive in a hostile microscopic world.
     9→3.2 Organelle Categories (40+ Organelles)
    10→3.2.1 Core Eukaryotic Organelles
    11→The Core Eukaryotic Organelles represent the fundamental machinery of advanced cellular life. The
    12→Nucleus serves as the command center, storing DNA and enabling evolution itself, making it one of the
    13→most critical organelles for progression. The Nucleolus enhances ribosome production by 15%,
    14→accelerating protein synthesis. The Rough Endoplasmic Reticulum and Smooth Endoplasmic Reticulum
    15→specialize in protein and lipid synthesis respectively, each providing a 10% boost to their respective
    16→outputs. The Golgi Apparatus packages proteins for transport, improving overall cellular efficiency by
    17→10%. Mitochondria generate ATP for energy, providing a 10% speed boost, while Peroxisomes handle
    18→detoxification and offer 5% toxin resistance. The Lysosome grants +3 attack through digestive
    19→enzymes, and the Vacuole provides 50% additional storage capacity. Ribosomes enhance protein
    20→synthesis by 5%, and the Cytoskeleton variants improve movement precision and cell division
    21→capabilities.
    22→3.2.2 Movement Organelles
    23→Movement Organelles determine how effectively your cell can navigate its environment and escape
    24→predators. The Flagellum provides a significant +20 speed boost through whip-like propulsion. Cilia
    25→offer +15 speed along with the ability to manipulate fluid currents, useful for gathering resources. The
    26→Pseudopod grants +10 speed and enables engulfment of smaller cells, opening up predatory gameplay

    29→options. These movement options create meaningful strategic choices between raw speed,
    30→maneuverability, and feeding capabilities.
    31→3.2.3 Attack/Defense Organelles
    32→Attack and Defense Organelles define your cell's combat capabilities and survival strategies. The Pilus
    33→provides +5 attack with puncture capability, ideal for aggressive cells. The Toxin Vacuole deals damage
    34→over time to enemies, creating a debuff-focused combat style. The Cell Wall offers +2 defense as a
    35→passive protective barrier. Bioluminescence provides a unique utility combination, attracting
    36→resources while confusing predators with light displays. These options allow players to specialize their
    37→cells as aggressive hunters, defensive survivors, or resource-gathering specialists.
    38→3.2.4 Energy/Specialty Organelles
    39→Energy and Specialty Organelles provide alternative metabolic strategies. The Chloroplast generates
    40→passive glucose during daytime, enabling photosynthetic gameplay. The Thermoplast provides health
    41→regeneration and heat energy utilization for cells in thermal environments. The Chemoplast allows
    42→energy extraction from chemicals, opening up extreme environment colonization. These organelles
    43→fundamentally change how players approach resource gathering, shifting from active hunting to
    44→passive collection or environmental exploitation.
    45→3.2.5 Plant-like, Animal-like, Fungal, and Protist-like Organelles
    46→Specialized organelle paths allow cells to evolve toward specific archetypes. Plant-like organelles
    47→include the Central Vacuole for massive storage and turgor pressure, Chromoplast for pigment
    48→storage, and Amyloplast for starch storage. Animal-like organelles feature Centrioles and Centrosome
    49→for faster cell division, and Acrosome for enhanced attack capabilities. Fungal organelles include
    50→Woronin Bodies for damage sealing and Spitzenkorper for directed growth. Protist-like organelles
    51→offer Contractile Vacuole for osmoregulation, Eyespot for light sensing, Trichocysts for defense
    52→discharge, and Hydrogenosome for anaerobic ATP generation.
    53→3.3 Archetype Specializations
    54→Each of the seven archetypes begins with unique cellular advantages that influence gameplay from the
    55→earliest moments. Terrestrial cells have balanced stats suitable for land-based evolution. Aquatic cells
    56→excel in water environments with enhanced swimming capabilities. Amphibious cells possess
    57→adaptations for both environments. Fungal cells gain bonuses to decomposition and research.
    58→Arboreal cells benefit from photosynthesis-related bonuses. Subterranean cells have enhanced
    59→burrowing and sensing capabilities. Aerial cells, while unusual at this stage, possess lightweight
    60→structures that will later enable flight.

    63→4. Creature Stage
    64→4.1 Overview
    65→The Creature Stage represents the transition from single-celled organism to multicellular complexity.
    66→Players control an evolving creature navigating a 2D world, hunting for food, avoiding predators,
    67→finding mates, and gradually developing the adaptations that will define their species. This stage
    68→features 27 technologies across 3 development paths, with 6 unique archetypal technologies that
    69→shape the evolutionary trajectory of your species. The core mechanics emphasize adaptation
    70→selection, hunting and gathering, nest building, and reproduction mechanics.
    71→4.2 Development Paths
    72→4.2.1 Movement Adaptations Path
    73→The Movement Adaptations path focuses on locomotion and environmental traversal. This path
    74→begins with Legs providing +15 speed for terrestrial movement, the most common starting point for
    75→land-based creatures. Wings unlock aerial capabilities with +25 speed and glide ability, opening
    76→entirely new strategic options for resource gathering and escape. Fins provide +20 water speed for
    77→aquatic-focused evolution. Tail offers +5 speed with improved balance, useful for climbing and
    78→jumping. Webbed Feet enable amphibious gameplay with +15 swimming speed. Hooves provide +10
    79→land speed with the ability to traverse rough terrain that would slow other creatures. This path defines
    80→how your creature interacts with the physical world and what environments become accessible.
    81→4.2.2 Combat Adaptations Path
    82→The Combat Adaptations path determines your creature's effectiveness in hunting and self-defense.
    83→Claws provide +8 attack as versatile melee weapons suitable for both hunting and combat. Teeth offer
    84→+5 attack with consistent damage output. Horns grant +6 attack with charge attack capability, enabling
    85→hit-and-run tactics. Venom Glands introduce poison damage over time, creating a debuff-focused
    86→combat style that weakens prey or predators. Spines provide thorns damage to attackers, turning
    87→defense into offense. Beaks offer +4 attack with precision strikes, useful for targeting specific weak
    88→points. This path creates meaningful choices between burst damage, sustained damage, and defensive
    89→capabilities.
    90→4.2.3 Survival Adaptations Path
    91→The Survival Adaptations path encompasses defense, senses, and special abilities that enhance
    92→survivability. Armor provides +10 defense but may slow movement. Camouflage offers 15% stealth for
    93→ambush hunting and predator avoidance. Shell grants +15 defense at the cost of -5 speed, ideal for
    94→slow, defensive creatures. Spikes provide 20% damage reflection, punishing attackers. Mimicry allows
    95→your creature to appear as other species, enabling deception-based gameplay. Sense adaptations
    96→include enhanced Vision (+30%), Echolocation for detecting hidden creatures, Thermal Sense for
    97→detecting warm bodies, and Electroreception for sensing life in water. Special abilities include

   429→The FTL Systems path develops faster-than-light propulsion. FTL Navigation establishes basic
   430→interstellar travel. Hyperdrive Mk1, Warp Drive Mk2, Jump Drive, Hyperlane Mastery, Wormhole
   431→Stabilization, Quantum Drives, Transwarp Conduits, and Dimensional Shift progress from basic FTL to
   432→instantaneous travel across the galaxy, with FTL speeds increasing from 1.0 to 15.0.

   435→9.2.2 Ship Design Path (7 Technologies)
   436→The Ship Design path develops capital ship hulls. Corvette Hulls, Destroyer Hulls, Cruiser Hulls,
   437→Battleship Hulls, Titan Hulls, Colossus Hulls, and Juggernaut Hulls enable progressively larger and more
   438→powerful warships, culminating in Juggernaut-class mobile shipyards.
   439→9.2.3 Weapons Systems Path (10 Technologies)
   440→The Weapons Systems path develops offensive capabilities. Laser Weapons Mk1, Kinetic Weapons,
   441→Missile Systems, Plasma Weapons, Railguns, Torpedoes, Particle Lances, Giga Cannons, Matter
   442→Disintegrators, and Dimensional Weapons progress from conventional weapons to reality-altering
   443→armaments.
   444→9.2.4 Shields & Armor Path (9 Technologies)
   445→The Shields & Armor path develops defensive systems. Shield Generators Mk1, Armor Plating Mk1,
   446→Deflectors, Neutronium Armor, Regenerative Shields, Nanite Armor, Psionic Barriers, Crystalline
   447→Armor, and Quantum Shields progress from basic protection to near-invulnerability.
   448→9.2.5 Megastructures Path (10 Technologies)
   449→The Megastructures path enables galactic-scale engineering. Mega-Engineering establishes
   450→construction foundations. Orbital Habitats Mk1, Dyson Swarm, Science Nexus, Sentry Array, Matter
   451→Decompressor, Mega Shipyard, Ring World, Dyson Sphere, and Galactic Wonder represent
   452→increasingly ambitious projects that generate massive resources, research, or victory progress.
   453→9.2.6 Diplomacy Path (8 Technologies)
   454→The Diplomacy path develops interstellar relations. Xenology enables first contact. Galactic Diplomacy,
   455→Federation Founding, Trade Agreements, Research Pacts, Galactic Council, Interstellar Law, and
   456→Galactic Federation create frameworks for peaceful cooperation or political domination.
   457→9.2.7 Economy Path (9 Technologies)
   458→The Economy path develops galactic-scale commerce. Interstellar Trade, Resource Extraction, Alloy
   459→Production, Consumer Goods, Galactic Markets, Deep Core Mining, Nanite Production, Dark Matter
   460→Refining, and Zero Point Energy generate the resources needed for galactic civilization.
   461→9.2.8 Science Path (9 Technologies)
   462→The Science path advances research capabilities. Research Labs, Physics Research, Society Research,
   463→Engineering Research, Research Institutes, Quantum Computing, Precursor Studies, Omega Theory,
   464→and Mind Over Matter provide research bonuses culminating in 75%+ improvements.
   465→9.2.9 Psionics Path (7 Technologies)
   466→The Psionics path develops mental powers. Psionic Theory unlocks this rare path. Telepathy,
   467→Telekinesis, Precognition, Psionic Shields, Mind Control, and Transcendence develop increasingly
   468→powerful mental abilities, culminating in the Psionic Ascension victory path.

   472→The Synthetic Evolution path develops machine integration. Robotics, Droid Workers, Synthetic

   475→9.2.11 Biological Evolution Path (6 Technologies)
   476→The Biological Evolution path develops genetic mastery. Genome Mapping, Gene Editing, Cloning,
   477→Advanced Genetics, Evolutionary Mastery, and Biological Ascension enable complete control over
   478→biological evolution, culminating in the Biological Ascension victory path.
   479→9.2.12 Crisis Response Path (4 Technologies)
   480→The Crisis Response path develops defenses against galactic threats. Crisis Contingency, Galactic
   481→Defense, Omega Weapons, and Reality Anchors provide bonuses against end-game crises that
   482→threaten galactic civilization.
   483→9.2.13 Ascension Paths (4 Technologies)
   484→The Ascension Paths technology tree enables victory through transcendence. The Conduit provides
   485→unity bonuses. Transcendence, Synthetic Perfection, and Evolutionary Apex unlock the three
   486→ascension victory conditions, each representing a different philosophical approach to ultimate
   487→evolution.
